SQL Budget vs. actual I compared a full year of budget vs. actual in SQL. A data quality check found duplicate entries that were distorting the result. −30% is the real result against budget, not −11% as it seemed See details →
Problem
Pre-tax income looked 11.1% below budget.
What I did
11 SQLite queries: budget vs. actual by account, cost center and month, plus a data quality check.

Python + DuckDB Retail working capital A pipeline that pulls the financial statements of 24 listed retailers from the CVM, checks data quality and crosses them with the central bank's Selic rate. 5.5% of revenue is the cost of carrying working capital in fashion, against a 13.1% EBIT margin See details →
Problem
How much of operating profit does working capital eat up when interest rates rise?
What I did
ETL on public data: 1.9 million rows from the CVM (2015–2025) and central bank series, transformed in SQL with DuckDB, with quality rules and 10 tests.
Result
With low rates, the median cash conversion cycle rose from 49 to 70 days; with high rates, it fell back to 45. In fashion, carrying working capital costs 5.5% of revenue.
Tools
Python · DuckDB · SQL · matplotlib
